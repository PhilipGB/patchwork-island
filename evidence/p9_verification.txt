Save compatibility
------------------
Schema remains version 1. CharacterPreset, OwnedOutfits and EquippedOutfit are
optional additive fields. A v1.0.x save with none of them loads as Boy +
Everyday with no purchases. Unknown/unowned customisation IDs are rejected by
validation rather than silently repaired.
